# People

House → People is the contact book.

Add a photo so it can say hello by name. A voice sample helps it tell who is speaking.

The first person can be marked as you (owner). Each person has a role: owner, family, kid, or guest. Kid is the same bound as Kids around.

Invite from People after the robot is [claimed](claim). Strangers stay in a separate pile until you claim them.

Camera stays on demand unless you turn that off in Behaviors.
