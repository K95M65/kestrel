# Kids and safety

Kids around (House → Behaviors) is a bound, not a vibe.

Mail, calendar, and computer-use stay off. Stories stay on. Camera stays on demand.

Quiet hours still apply. Safety numbers (speed, brightness, quiet hours) live on the body in `SAFETY.md` — they are not prompts.

Do not put the robot in charge of anything you would not trust a polite stranger with.
