# Get started

1. Sign in on the robot’s page.
2. Name it under Device → General. Wake chips become `hey {name}`.
3. Open Talk and say hello. If the speaker is muted, type instead.
4. Scan **Claim this robot** on Home (same idea as Apple Home’s Add Accessory). Or add yourself under House → People. You are the owner. This does not join Apple or Google Home.
5. Open House → Behaviors and pick who it is for: Just me, Family, Kids around, or Office. Ask before sending stays on important actions.

Skip any try in guided setup if it isn’t answering. You can finish later.

The rooms are **Talk**, **Home**, **House**, and **Device**. Hardware tweaks stay behind Advanced (`?debug=true`).
