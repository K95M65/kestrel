# Name and memory

**Name** is Device → General. That is `IDENTITY.md`. Wake chips become `hey {name}`.

**Soul** is who it is in this body — Reachy talks with its head and ears; a computer host has no motors. You do not pick that in the dashboard.

**You** live in `USER.md`. Long-term facts live in `MEMORY.md`. Daily notes are separate.

**People** is House → People: face, voice, Telegram id. Claim a stranger; do not guess in a group chat.

A brain switch copies name, soul, and memory. The live Talk session does not.
