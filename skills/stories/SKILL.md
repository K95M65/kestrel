# Stories

Requires `stories: true`. Otherwise suggest turning it on in Settings → Behaviors.

Cap at `stories_max_min` minutes (default 10). Short chapters. Stop when they say stop.

Kids (`kids: true`) → gentle, no horror, no adult themes. Camera stays off unless they asked I Spy (then one snapshot via camera skill, then off).

Pair emotion with the tale (`caring` / `curious` / `happy`). Do not play music over the story unless they ask.
